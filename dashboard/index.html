Token launch radar

What the number is

An unlaunched protocol, one with no CoinGecko id and no symbol, is valued as a launch claim: TVL times 0.22, times a structure scale, a tenure curve that peaks near 450 days listed without a token, a damped seven-day TVL change, chain breadth, and a small-TVL confidence haircut. A protocol that already records a token uses 0.018 instead of 0.22, times the share of TVL its market cap has not already covered and a season that shrinks as the listing gets older. Bridges, exchange books, liquid staking receipts, versioned names, and exchange-prefixed names are scaled down. No protocol slug is pinned to a rank.
